These are editorial scores, not measured demand. No local customer invoice or confirmed Unit4 deployment was found in the checked customer records. No live CRM read was performed in this no-secrets run. Buyer fit is an inference from purchasing-heavy businesses, with a deliberately low score. Search results show comparison coverage but do not establish search volume. Simfoni lacked verified annual-cost evidence in this run. CData Arc was rejected for the broader transport and integration requirements.

[Unit4 product and transfer description](https://prod-cms-eu-01.scanmarket.com/en/strategic-sourcing/spend-analytics/) describes supplier analysis, PO compliance, payment terms, tabular source data, connectors and configurable reporting. [Current Unit4 suite](https://www.unit4.com/products/source-to-contract-software) keeps spend analysis in Source-to-Contract. [Unit4's distinction between standalone analytics and Coda's built-in capability](https://www.unit4.com/products/financial-management-software/spend-analytics-lite) prevents conflating the two products.

The importer accepts mapped transaction-level flat files. No public universal Spend Analytics download schema or exact export-menu procedure was found. The cutover guide states that limit. Original purchasing records from the finance source are an alternative; vendor-normalised categories and supplier mappings must be reconciled separately.

The free base covers financial line history, supplier alias reconciliation, classification, concentration, unit-price comparisons, review actions, evidence and savings working papers. It does not reproduce the broader sourcing suite, managed classification service, external carbon or market datasets, live finance connections, payment-term optimisation or a hosted user interface. Unit4 can provide custom dashboards, so no claim is made that it cannot answer the ten questions shown here.

[Simfoni product](https://simfoni.com/spend-intelligence/) and [CData Arc current quote-based pricing](https://arc.cdata.com/order/) were checked for the shortlist. Neither is named as an exact product substitute on the comparison page.
